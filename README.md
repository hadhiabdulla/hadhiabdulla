## About me

I'm **Hadhi Abdulla**, a B.Tech CSE (Cyber Security) student. I like getting past the surface of a system: following the connections, testing an assumption, and finding out what changes when something goes wrong.

I learn best by turning curiosity into something practical: building, experimenting, testing ideas and working through the details. I'm developing my skills across cybersecurity, keeping an open mind about where the next problem will take me.

**Security is the thread. Systems are the playground.**

This GitHub is where I keep the code and the work behind it. My portfolio brings those projects together in a more interactive way. Have a look around, open a repository, and follow whatever catches your interest.
